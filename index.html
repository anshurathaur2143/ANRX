MY DELIVERY SYSTEM

How I take a project from problem to delivery.

Five steps. Every project.

01

Discover & Elicit

Talk to stakeholders. Understand the real problem, not just the request.

02

Analyze & Model

Define business rules, map processes, and remove ambiguity before anything is built.

03

Document & Validate

Write clear BRDs, user stories, and acceptance criteria that developers and QA can actually use.

04

Support Delivery

Stay connected with the team. Answer questions, manage changes, and keep scope clear.

05

Verify & Improve

Support UAT, track defects, confirm business sign-off, and capture what can be done better next time.
