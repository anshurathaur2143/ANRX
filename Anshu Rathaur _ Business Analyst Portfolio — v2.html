01 / Business context & analysis approach

The suite combines employee lifecycle workflows with hospital operations and safety tools. My BA focus is to turn policy-heavy needs into requirements that can be reviewed, built, tested and traced — clarifying actors, permissions, triggers, data, status transitions, approval ownership, exceptions, reporting and integrations before treating a requirement as delivery-ready.

BRD — what & why
  • Business objective, scope, stakeholders, assumptions, constraints and success measures.
  • High-level needs: reduce manual hand-offs, improve visibility, standardize policy execution and maintain auditability.
  • Out-of-scope items and dependencies called out before baseline approval.
FRD — expected system behaviour
  • Actors, preconditions, trigger, main flow, alternate/exception flows and postconditions.
  • Field-level validation, permissions, business rules, notifications, reports and integration behaviour.
  • Requirement IDs linked to stories, acceptance criteria and test coverage through traceability.

02 / Example traceability chain

Business need→BRD objective→FRD rule→User story + AC→Test case / UAT

Example: "Approved hours must feed payroll" → timesheet approval rule → integration requirement → story with AC → tests for approved, rejected, corrected and duplicate submissions.

CASE 02 / HMS
PATIENT FLOW · BED STATE · ROSTER
REGISTER→ASSIGN BED→DISCHARGE
HEALTHCARE · SAAS · HOSPITAL OPERATIONS

Hospital Management System

Initial scope: patient registration & billing, bed/room management, and doctor scheduling. The BA challenge is keeping patient, bed and roster states consistent across hand-offs.

As-is / to-be workflowBusiness rulesGap analysisQA acceptance
Open the full BA case study ↗

01 / Workflow steps

01Register patient

Capture demographics, insurance, consent; validate mandatory fields and duplicates.

02Estimate billing

Generate estimate from billing rules; surface missing coverage data.

03Assign bed

Show occupancy and readiness; prevent assignment to occupied or cleaning-in-progress beds.

04Schedule doctor

Match availability and specialty; flag duty-hour conflicts.

05Discharge & release

Record discharge summary; move bed through cleaning states before re-offering.

02 / Key business rules

  • A bed is assignable only when state is "available"; occupied/reserved/cleaning states block assignment.
  • Bed state must not flip to "ready" until all discharge and room-readiness steps complete.
  • Doctor scheduling must respect specialty, availability, duty hours and critical-care coverage.

03 / UAT scenarios

  • Register with all mandatory fields → record and estimate created.
  • Missing consent or insurance → configured validation response fires.
  • Assign available bed → attempt same bed for second patient → conflict blocked.
  • Discharge patient → bed follows cleaning states → not immediately shown as ready.
Resume-reported outcomes: 30% less manual bed-tracking effort and 90%+ first-pass QA acceptance.

E-commerce platform & integrations

Requirements across product catalog, cart, checkout, payments and order workflows for platforms serving 50,000+ users. BA challenge: define the data contract and behaviour when external services return delayed, invalid or failed responses.

API requirementsSQL validationUATRelease traceability

01 / Business flow

Browse catalog→Cart validation→Checkout→Payment response→Order status

02 / API requirements I baseline

  • Contract: endpoint purpose, method, required/optional fields, data types, authentication, response codes, versioning.
  • Failure path: declined payment, timeout, duplicate callback, inventory change — each must produce a defined state and user message.
  • Idempotency: retries must not create duplicate charges or orders.

03 / SQL validation approach

Example: one order with two line items must not be double-counted after joining multiple payment events. Aggregate at the correct grain then reconcile to a trusted report.

04 / Reported impact

15% reduction in cart abandonment and 12+ feature enhancements contributed across releases.

CONTENTAI ASSISTAPPROVAL
AI-ENABLED ELEARNING · DIGITAL PUBLISHING

UMGC AI-based content platform

Requirements for an AI-enabled platform supporting eLearning material creation and layout automation across 25+ courses. Key BA challenge: keep AI-assisted edits reviewable, reversible and governed through the content lifecycle.

Requirement gatheringFunctional requirementsWorkflow designReporting

01 / Functional workflow

01Create / import

Author or import course content; capture title, owner, course, version, status.

02AI-assisted edit

Request summarization, grammar correction, text optimization or layout assistance.

03Review changes

Compare proposed output with source; accept or reject; preserve editable version.

04Version & approve

Track revisions, author, reviewer, timestamps and approval status before publish.

05Preview & publish

Validate layout and required fields; record final status; report throughput.

02 / Key acceptance criteria

  • AI summary returns a proposed version — source is never silently overwritten.
  • Only authorized roles can publish approved versions; draft or returned content cannot bypass approval.
  • Version history identifies who changed what and when.

03 / Reported impact

25% faster content turnaround and workflow tracking across 100+ content pieces per month.
