CORE RESPONSIBILITIES

Four areas of BA book.

These are the core activities and artifacts I use to turn business goals into work that product, engineering, and QA teams can understand and validate.

01 / DISCOVER

Requirement gathering and analysis

Understand the problem before defining the solution. Explore business goals, current workflows, pain points, rules, constraints, and expected outcomes.

  • Conduct stakeholder interviews and requirement workshops.
  • Review existing documents and current-state processes.
  • Analyze gaps, dependencies, assumptions, and impact.
  • Clarify and prioritize functional and non-functional needs.
Requirement logGap analysisImpact analysis
02 / COLLABORATE

Stakeholder management

Build a shared understanding across business users, product owners, developers, QA, and other delivery partners.

  • Identify stakeholder roles, needs, and decision owners.
  • Facilitate discussions and resolve conflicting expectations.
  • Share decisions, actions, risks, and open questions.
  • Confirm scope, priorities, and requirement sign-off.
Stakeholder registerMeeting notesDecision log
03 / DOCUMENT

BRDs, FRDs, user stories and acceptance criteria

Create documentation that is clear for stakeholders and specific enough for delivery and testing.

  • BRD: business objectives, scope, needs, and success measures.
  • FRD: functional behaviour, rules, validations, and exceptions.
  • User stories: user, capability, and business value.
  • Acceptance criteria: observable, testable expected outcomes.
BRD / FRDUser storiesAcceptance criteriaRTM
04 / VALIDATE

Process mapping and UAT coordination

Make workflows visible and coordinate business validation so the delivered solution matches agreed requirements.

  • Map current-state and future-state workflows using BPMN or flowcharts.
  • Identify handoffs, decision points, exceptions, and bottlenecks.
  • Prepare UAT scenarios, test data, and expected results.
  • Track defects, coordinate retesting, and support sign-off.
